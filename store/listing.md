**App name (max 30):** FX Trade Journal

**Short description (max 80):**
Trading journal for forex: import your MT5 history and see what really works.

**Full description:**
FX Trade Journal turns your trading history into a clear dashboard, so you can see which pairs, days and sessions make you money and which ones cost you.

Get your trades in without typing:
• Import the history report you save from MetaTrader 5 (HTML or Excel), or a CSV file
• Or connect your account through MetaApi and sync with one tap (needs your own MetaApi token)
• Works with Strategy Tester backtest reports too, so you can review your Expert Advisors

See your performance at a glance:
• Total, long and short net P&L, with gross profit vs. gross loss
• Win rate, profit rate, profit factor, expectancy, average win and loss
• Distribution of gains and losses
• Balance curve with a 20-trade average, and a weekly curve
• P&L for this month, each quarter, year to date and last year
• Max drawdown and your longest winning and losing streaks
• Win % by pair, weekday or trading session
• Your last 20 trades compared with your overall stats: improving or declining
• Full trade log with commission and swap
• Filter everything by pair, side and date range

Private by design: imported files are read on your phone and never uploaded.
