FX Trade Journal privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

FX Trade Journal ("the app") helps traders review their trading history. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Contact: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

Trading data

When you import a MetaTrader 5 history report, the file is read on your device only. The trades it contains are stored on your device so the dashboard can show them next time. They are not sent to us.

Connecting a trading account

If you choose "Connect MT5 account", you enter a MetaApi access token, your trading account number, broker server name and investor (read-only) password. The app sends these to MetaApi (metaapi.cloud, operated by Agilium Trade), a third-party service that connects to your broker and returns your trade history. MetaApi's own privacy policy applies to that data. The app stores your MetaApi token, account number and server name on your device. It does not store your password. We do not receive any of this data. We recommend using an investor (read-only) password, which cannot place or close trades.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how ads are shown and tapped, to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Deleting your data

Use "Clear imported trades" and "Disconnect" in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it has stored on your device. To delete the account copy held by MetaApi, remove the account in your MetaApi dashboard.

Children

The app is meant for adults who trade financial markets and is not directed at children under 18.
